Your Journey In Circle Pune

From your first invitation to building world-class products together.

STAGE 01

Discover & Apply

Submit your application with your proof of work, GitHub/Figma portfolio, or startup deck. Applications are reviewed weekly by community stewards.

STAGE 02

Connect & Meet

Get access to the private Circle Pune Discord & WhatsApp hubs. Attend your first rooftop coffee or beer mixer in Baner or Koregaon Park.

STAGE 03

Collaborate & Sprint

Find technical co-founders, hire top local product designers, and form hackathon sprint teams for weekend hackathons.

STAGE 04

Build & Launch

Ship your product live at Circle Pune Demo Days in front of 200+ local builders and venture investors.

STAGE 05

Grow & Mentor

Pay it forward by mentoring next-generation campus founders and hosting sub-circle micro-events.
